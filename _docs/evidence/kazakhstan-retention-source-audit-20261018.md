These facts may affect which tax-record rules apply and require review against the operative Code text.
- **UNRESOLVED — document mapping:** No approved inventory maps Pin records to statutory classes (for example, invoices, bank/payment evidence, expense support, accounting registers, tax forms, or ordinary personal conversation). Do not treat a broad “financial” label as sufficient classification.
- **UNRESOLVED — clocks and holds:** Applicable retention triggers, audit/review or litigation holds, asset-related rules, and other exceptions have not been verified from the operative provisions or mapped into a product rule.
- **UNRESOLVED — sector rules:** No regulated financial, archival, or other sector has been selected. Sector-specific retention schedules may impose different requirements if the operator later enters one.
- **UNRESOLVED — privacy/deletion interaction:** This source audit does not assess personal-data law, user deletion rights, cross-border processing, or conflicts between statutory recordkeeping and deletion. Those need separate primary-source analysis after the operator and data-flow are known.
- **PROPOSED:** None. No numeric TTL, default hold, user-facing promise, or deletion exception is proposed by this document.

## Gate result

**Not resolved; no compliance pass.** Retrieved primary sources establish that the current Tax Code contains Articles 65 and 206 and that Order No. 279-НҚ exists, but this pass did not retrieve the operative Tax Code provisions or the order’s annex rows needed to verify their periods and triggers. The evidence therefore does not establish a numeric retention duration for Pin’s financial information or all user data. Keep the product retention setting unset until the applicable official text, operator form, tax context, record inventory, conditions, and privacy/deletion interaction are reviewed and the owner explicitly approves policy.

## Research limits

Research was bounded to primary Kazakhstan legal/government sources listed above. The current Adilet text for Law No. 234 was intermittently unavailable to page retrieval, so article references were cross-checked against Adilet’s indexed current text. The KGD-hosted Tax Code pages retrieved here exposed contents rather than the operative Article 65/206 bodies; the Order No. 279-НҚ annex rows were likewise not independently fetched. No source was found in this bounded pass that extends a tax-document rule to an entire personal assistant conversation or profile. This is a search limit, not proof that no other rule could apply.
