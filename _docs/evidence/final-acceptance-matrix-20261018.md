## Scope boundaries

The four hosted lane-stop/recovery probes and reboot observation establish those specific monitoring behaviors; synthetic fault fixtures establish local projection behavior only. Neither establishes external paging, automatic provider reconciliation, automatic repair, a queue-capacity guarantee, or live payment behavior. The latest isolated restore/reconciliation sequence completed through target release, a successful startup check, and guarded cleanup, with an exact baseline catalog afterward and no session termination. Its measured snapshot lag and isolated recovery interval were within the adopted 24-hour and four-hour bounds for that rehearsal. The capture window had zero relevant events, so this does not establish provider-history completeness, nonzero hosted post-backup deletion replay, an actual outage/replacement-host recovery, or live cutover.

The formal model-quality/capacity/economics benchmark and actual Stars requirement are removed from acceptance, not deferred. Kazakhstan is the stated jurisdiction, but legal entity/IP/TOO form, provider eligibility, statutory retention, domain/public launch readiness, and owner final acceptance are not verified by this matrix. No legal waiver or policy is inferred. Issues 21, 23–26, and 28 remain open in their owning scopes. This matrix is a traceability aid only, not an acceptance or release decision.
